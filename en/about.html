About Us

The vision and passion behind P9 Wheels.

Our Story

Born from the renowned Felgenwerk Andelfingen, P9 Wheels represents the absolute pinnacle in custom forged wheels. What began as a passion for perfect automotive refinement has evolved into an exclusive brand.

We believe that wheels are not just accessories, but the true character of a vehicle. Each of our designs is forged with the highest precision to unite aesthetics, performance, and exclusivity without compromise.

Our Values

Swiss Precision

The highest manufacturing standards and uncompromising quality control for every single wheel.

Exclusive Design

Timeless aesthetics paired with state-of-the-art technology for a distinctive and premium look.

Maximum Safety

Forged aerospace-grade aluminum for extreme structural strength at minimum weight.

Swiss Precision. Unmistakable Design. Uncompromising Performance.
